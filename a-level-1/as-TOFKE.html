What happens inside the spectrometer

Every calculation on the sheet comes from two facts. In the acceleration area every 1+ ion gets the same kinetic energy. In the flight tube there is no field, so each ion keeps a constant velocity. Fire a bunch of ions and watch the lighter ones arrive first.

Units: the trap in every question

KE is in joules, so mass must be in kg and velocity in m s−1. A mass number tells you the mass of a mole of ions in grams. You need two conversions to get the mass of one ion in kg.

The electron knocked off during ionisation has a tiny mass. So the mass of one mole of X+ ions in g is the same number as the mass number.
