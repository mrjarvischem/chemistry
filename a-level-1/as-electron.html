The toolkit

Electrons are arranged in shells (energy levels), which are split into sub-shells (s, p, d), which are made of orbitals. An orbital is a region that holds up to two electrons with opposite spins.

Reading the notation

3p4
  • 3 = the shell (energy level)
  • p = the type of sub-shell
  • 4 = electrons in that sub-shell

The superscripts always add up to the number of electrons. Sulfur: 1s2 2s2 2p6 3s2 3p4 → 2 + 2 + 6 + 2 + 4 = 16.

Noble gas shorthand: replace the electrons of the previous noble gas with its symbol in square brackets. Sulfur = [Ne] 3s2 3p4.

The filling order

Sub-shells fill in order of energy. The 4s sub-shell is lower in energy than 3d, so it fills first.

Totals show the electrons used once each sub-shell is full.
