Testing for gases

GasTestPositive result
hydrogenburning (lit) splint held at the open end of the test tubeburns with a pop (squeaky pop)
oxygenglowing splint inserted into the test tubesplint relights
carbon dioxidebubble the gas through (or shake with) limewater (calcium hydroxide solution)limewater turns milky (cloudy)
chlorinedamp litmus paper put into the gaslitmus paper is bleached (turns white)
Glowing splint (oxygen) ≠ lit / burning splint (hydrogen). The litmus paper for chlorine must be damp. Blue litmus may turn red briefly before it bleaches.
