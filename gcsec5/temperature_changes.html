Walkthrough 2 · Investigating temperature changes (required practical 4)

AQA GCSE Chemistry 4.5.1.1 · Required practical 4 (Combined Science RP 10) · about 30 minutes

Required practical: temperature changes

Investigate how the volume of sodium hydroxide added to hydrochloric acid affects the temperature change.

The polystyrene cup is a good insulator and the lid stops energy escaping, so less energy is lost to the surroundings. The beaker stops the cup falling over.

Do the experiment

30 cm³ of hydrochloric acid is in the cup. Add sodium hydroxide 5 cm³ at a time, stir, and record the highest temperature after each addition.

Draw two straight lines of best fit — one through the rising points, one through the falling points. Where they cross gives the maximum temperature and the volume needed to neutralise the acid.

Variables and improving the results

Quiz and exam practice

Summary

  • Polystyrene cup + lid reduce energy transfer to the surroundings.
  • Independent: volume of NaOH (or type/amount of reactant); dependent: temperature change.
  • Control: volume and concentration of acid, concentration of NaOH, starting temperature, same cup.
  • Two lines of best fit cross at the maximum temperature.
