2. A full exam question, start to finish

November 2020 Paper 1 Q6 (10 marks in total)

Methanol can be manufactured in a reversible reaction: CO(g) + 2H₂(g) ⇌ CH₃OH(g), ΔH = −91 kJ mol⁻¹

PART 1 · Q6.2 (4 marks)

A 0.230 mol sample of CO is mixed with hydrogen in a 1:2 mol ratio and allowed to reach equilibrium at temperature T. At equilibrium the mixture contains 0.120 mol CO. The total pressure is 1.04 × 10⁴ kPa. Calculate the partial pressure of hydrogen.

kPa
H₂: 0.460 − 0.220 = 0.240 mol; CH₃OH 0.110 mol; total 0.470 mol → x(H₂) = 0.511 → p(H₂) = 5310 kPa
PART 2 · Q6.3 (2 marks)

Give an expression for Kp for this reaction and state its units.


Units:
M1 Kp = p(CH₃OH)p(H₂)² × p(CO) (do not allow square brackets)   M2 kPa⁻² or Pa⁻²
PART 3 · Q6.4 (2 marks)

Some more CO is added and the mixture reaches equilibrium again at temperature T. Effect on the partial pressure of methanol, and on Kp?

M1 p(CH₃OH) increases   M2 Kp: no effect
PART 4 · Q6.5 (2 marks)

State the effect, if any, of adding a catalyst on the value of Kp. Explain your answer.

M1 No effect   M2 It increases the rate of the forward and backward reactions equally / doesn't affect the position of equilibrium

Extension

Use your answers to calculate Kp at temperature T.

p(CO) = 0.120/0.470 × 1.04 × 10⁴ = 2655 kPa; p(H₂) = 5311 kPa; p(CH₃OH) = 0.110/0.470 × 1.04 × 10⁴ = 2434 kPa
Kp = 24345311² × 2655 = 3.25 × 10⁻⁸ kPa⁻²
