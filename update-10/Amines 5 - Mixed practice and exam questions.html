EXAMAQA A-level Paper 2, June 2022, Q10

Methylbenzene is used in two routes. Route 1: step 1 → 4-nitromethylbenzene; step 2 → amine A, 4-methylphenylamine (CH₃C₆H₄NH₂); step 3 → CH₃C₆H₄NHCOCH₃. Route 2: step 4 → C₆H₅CH₂Cl; step 5 → amine B, C₆H₅CH₂NH₂.

10.1

State the reagents for step 1 and for step 2. [3 marks]

Step 1: concentrated HNO₃ and concentrated H₂SO₄
Step 2: Sn and (concentrated) HCl (then NaOH)
10.2

Name the mechanism for step 3. [1 mark]

Nucleophilic addition–elimination
10.3

Name the reagent for step 4 and state a necessary condition. [2 marks]

Chlorine (Cl₂); UV light
10.4

Explain why the yield of B in step 5 is less than the yield of A in step 2. [2 marks]

In step 5 (C₆H₅CH₂Cl + NH₃), amine B is also a nucleophile and reacts further with C₆H₅CH₂Cl
giving secondary/tertiary amines and a quaternary salt – a mixture. Reduction in step 2 gives only one product.
10.5

Explain why amine B is a stronger base than amine A. [2 marks]

In A the lone pair on N is delocalised into the ring; in B it is not (CH₂ in between).
So the lone pair on N in B is more available to accept H+.

EXAM 1AQA A-level Paper 2, June 2024, Q10.2, 10.4–10.5

1,2-dimethylbenzene is converted in Step 1 to 1,2-dimethyl-4-nitrobenzene, then in Step 2 to 3,4-dimethylphenylamine. (a) State the two reagents for Step 1 and give an equation for forming the reactive intermediate. (b) State the reagent(s) for Step 2. (c) State a possible use for the amine formed. [4 marks]

(a) concentrated HNO₃ and concentrated H₂SO₄; HNO₃ + 2H₂SO₄ → NO₂+ + H₃O+ + 2HSO₄− (or HNO₃ + H₂SO₄ → NO₂+ + HSO₄− + H₂O)
(b) Sn and (concentrated) HCl
(c) making dyes (or medicines)
EXAM 2AQA A-level Paper 2, June 2018, Q10.1

Three infrared spectra belong to a primary amine, a tertiary amine and a nitrile. Spectrum A has a sharp peak at about 2250 cm−¹. Spectrum B has no absorption between 3300 and 3500 cm−¹. Spectrum C has absorptions at about 3300–3400 cm−¹. Match each spectrum to the compound. [1 mark]

Primary amine: C (N–H stretch 3300–3500)
Tertiary amine: B (no N–H bond)
Nitrile: A (C≡N at 2220–2260)
EXAM 3AQA A-level Paper 2, June 2019, Q01.3

1,6-diaminohexane can be made in a two-stage synthesis from 1,4-dibromobutane. Suggest the reagent and a condition for each stage. [3 marks]

Stage 1: KCN in aqueous ethanol (reflux) → NC(CH₂)₄CN
Stage 2: H₂ with Ni catalyst (or LiAlH₄ in dry ether) → H₂N(CH₂)₆NH₂
