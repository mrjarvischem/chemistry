Section 1

The two titration equations

Potassium manganate(VII) is used to titrate reducing agents such as Fe2+ and C2O42− (ethanedioate). Combine the half-equations so the electrons cancel, then read off the mole ratio.

MnO₄⁻ with Fe²⁺
1
reduction
{MnO4^- + 8H^+ + 5e^- -> Mn^2+ + 4H2O}
2
oxidation × 5
{5Fe^2+ -> 5Fe^3+ + 5e^-}
3
overall
{MnO4^- + 8H^+ + 5Fe^2+ -> Mn^2+ + 4H2O + 5Fe^3+}

MnO₄⁻ : Fe²⁺ = 1 : 5

MnO₄⁻ with C₂O₄²⁻
1
reduction × 2
{2MnO4^- + 16H^+ + 10e^- -> 2Mn^2+ + 8H2O}
2
oxidation × 5
{5C2O4^2- -> 10CO2 + 10e^-}
3
overall
{2MnO4^- + 16H^+ + 5C2O4^2- -> 2Mn^2+ + 8H2O + 10CO2}

MnO₄⁻ : C₂O₄²⁻ = 2 : 5

Doing the titration

The KMnO4 goes in the burette. The flask contains the Fe2+ solution and an excess of dilute sulfuric acid. MnO4− is purple and Mn2+ is almost colourless, so the reaction is self-indicating.

Titrating ethanedioate. Warm the flask to about 60 °C – the reaction is slow at first because two negative ions must collide. It then speeds up because the Mn2+ formed is an autocatalyst (see Autocatalysis by Mn²⁺).
Section 3

The calculation method

① Moles of MnO₄⁻

n = c × V/1000 from the titre

② Use the ratio

× 5 for Fe2+; × 5/2 for C2O42−

③ Scale up if needed

e.g. 25.0 cm3 taken from 250 cm3: × 10

④ Answer the question

concentration = n ÷ V; mass = n × M; % = mass ÷ sample × 100

Section 5

Harder problems

Try each one on paper first, then open it to check your working.

1 · Water of crystallisation in FeSO₄·xH₂O

3.475 g of FeSO4·xH2O was made up to 250 cm3. 25.0 cm3 portions needed 25.00 cm3 of 0.0100 mol dm−3 KMnO4. Find x.

1
n(MnO4−) = 0.0100 × 25.00/1000 = 2.50 × 10−4 mol
2
n(Fe2+) = 5 × 2.50 × 10−4 = 1.25 × 10−3 mol in 25 cm3; × 10 = 1.25 × 10−2 mol
3
Mr = 3.475 ÷ 0.0125 = 278; FeSO4 = 151.9
4
x = (278 − 151.9) ÷ 18.0 = 7
2 · Hydrated ethanedioic acid, H₂C₂O₄·xH₂O

1.575 g was made up to 250 cm3. 25.0 cm3 portions needed 25.00 cm3 of 0.0200 mol dm−3 KMnO4. Find x.

1
n(MnO4−) = 5.00 × 10−4; n(C2O42−) = 5.00 × 10−4 × 5/2 = 1.25 × 10−3
2
× 10 = 1.25 × 10−2 mol; Mr = 1.575 ÷ 0.0125 = 126
3
H2C2O4 = 90.0, so x = 36.0 ÷ 18.0 = 2
3 · Dichromate: a "similar redox reaction"

{Cr2O7^2- + 14H^+ + 6Fe^2+ -> 2Cr^3+ + 7H2O + 6Fe^3+}. 25.0 cm3 of Fe2+ needed 18.75 cm3 of 0.0200 mol dm−3 K2Cr2O7. Find [Fe2+].

1
n(Cr2O72−) = 3.75 × 10−4; ratio 1 : 6, n(Fe2+) = 2.25 × 10−3
2
[Fe2+] = 2.25 × 10−3 ÷ 0.0250 = 0.0900 mol dm−3
4 · Hydrogen peroxide, diluted first

{2MnO4^- + 6H^+ + 5H2O2 -> 2Mn^2+ + 8H2O + 5O2}. 10.0 cm3 of H2O2 was diluted to 250 cm3. 25.0 cm3 needed 23.50 cm3 of 0.0200 mol dm−3 KMnO4. Find the original concentration.

1
n(MnO4−) = 4.70 × 10−4; n(H2O2) = × 5/2 = 1.175 × 10−3
2
diluted: 1.175 × 10−3 ÷ 0.0250 = 0.0470 mol dm−3
3
dilution factor 250 ÷ 10.0 = 25, so original = 1.18 mol dm−3
