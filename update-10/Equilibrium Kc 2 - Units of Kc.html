Equilibrium (Kc) 2 – Units of Kc

AQA A-level Chemistry · Equilibria walkthrough

1. Working out the units of Kc

Kc doesn't have fixed units. They depend on the expression, so you work them out every time.

  1. Write the Kc expression.
  2. Replace every concentration with its units, (mol dm⁻³), keeping the powers.
  3. Count how many lots of (mol dm⁻³) are on the top and on the bottom, and cancel.
  4. Write what's left. Anything left on the bottom moves to the top by changing the sign of its powers.

Interactive: units machine

Worked example

June 2018 Paper 2 Q4.2

2A + B ⇌ 3C + D   Give the expression for Kc and its units.

  1. Kc = [C]³ [D][A]² [B]
  2. Units = (mol dm⁻³)³ (mol dm⁻³)(mol dm⁻³)² (mol dm⁻³) = (mol dm⁻³)⁴(mol dm⁻³)³
  3. Cancel three from top and bottom: one (mol dm⁻³) is left on top.
  4. Units = mol dm⁻³

2. The shortcut: count the powers

Add up the powers on the top, add up the powers on the bottom, and subtract:

n = (total power on top) − (total power on bottom)

units = (mol dm⁻³)n

nUnits of Kc
+2mol² dm⁻⁶
+1mol dm⁻³
0no units
−1mol⁻¹ dm³
−2mol⁻² dm⁶

The power on dm is always −3 × the power on mol. That's a handy check.

Common errors: getting the signs the wrong way round (mol⁻¹ dm³ when it should be mol dm⁻³); writing dm³ as dm⁻³; counting the number of substances instead of adding the powers; giving units when they all cancel (write "no units").

3. Past-paper practice

QUICK CHECK 2 November 2020 AS Paper 2 Q8.1 (2 marks)

C₂H₅OH(g) + H₂O(g) ⇌ 2CO(g) + 4H₂(g)   Give an expression for Kc and state its units.

Kc = [CO]² [H₂]⁴[C₂H₅OH] [H₂O]
Top: 2 + 4 = 6. Bottom: 1 + 1 = 2. n = 6 − 2 = 4
Units = (mol dm⁻³)⁴ = mol⁴ dm⁻¹²
QUICK CHECK 3 June 2022 Paper 2 Q2.2

2CHClF₂(g) ⇌ C₂F₄(g) + 2HCl(g)   What are the units of Kc?

Kc = [C₂F₄] [HCl]²[CHClF₂]²   n = 3 − 2 = 1 → mol dm⁻³
QUICK CHECK 4 June 2017 Paper 2 Q5.3

2CH₃COOH(l) + HOCH₂CH₂OH(l) ⇌ C₆H₁₀O₄(l) + 2H₂O(l)   What are the units of Kc?

Top: 1 + 2 = 3. Bottom: 2 + 1 = 3. n = 0, so everything cancels: no units.
This matters later: when n = 0 the volume cancels too (Walkthrough 6).

Reversing the equation: value and units

When you flip the equation, the new Kc is 1 / old Kc, and every sign in the units flips too. mol⁻² dm⁶ becomes mol² dm⁻⁶.

QUICK CHECK 5 June 2018 AS Paper 2 Q8.4 (2 marks)

For CO(g) + 2H₂(g) ⇌ CH₃OH(g), assume Kc = 0.825 mol⁻² dm⁶ at temperature T.
Find the value and units of Kc for CH₃OH(g) ⇌ CO(g) + 2H₂(g) at temperature T.

Value:
Units:
Kc(reverse) = 1 / 0.825 = 1.21
Units flip: mol⁻² dm⁶ → mol² dm⁻⁶
QUICK CHECK 6 June 2022 AS Paper 1 Q7.5 (2 marks)

At 745 K, 3H₂(g) + N₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g) has Kc = 0.118 mol⁻² dm⁶.
Calculate Kc at 745 K for 2NH₃(g) ⇌ 3H₂(g) + N₂(g) and state its units.

Value:
1 / 0.118 = 8.47 mol² dm⁻⁶
