Why Complexes Are Coloured

Why are transition metal complexes coloured?

White light shines through a solution of the complex. Photons whose energy matches the gap between the split d orbitals are absorbed. The rest reach the screen, and that mix is the colour we see.

d-orbital energy levels

How it works

  1. In the free ion, all five 3d orbitals have the same energy.
  2. Ligands bond to the ion and split the d orbitals into two energy levels.
  3. A photon is absorbed when its energy equals the gap: ΔE = hν = hc/λ. An electron is promoted to the higher level.
  4. The photons that aren’t absorbed are transmitted. We see the colour of the transmitted light.
