Multi-step titration calculations

Work backwards from what you know. Step through each worked example, then try one with new numbers.

knownunknown / reactedleft over (excess)

    Standard solution: the method on one card

    1. Map the chain: solid → made up to 250 cm³ → 25.0 cm³ pipetted → titrated.
    2. The burette solution is the known. Write the equation and the ratio as known : unknown.
    3. n(known) = c × V ÷ 1000
    4. Use the ratio → n(unknown) in the 25.0 cm³ portion.
    5. × 10 → n(unknown) in the 250 cm³ flask = n in the solid weighed out.
    6. Finish: m = n × Mr → % purity, or Mr = m ÷ n (→ x).

    Back titration: the method on one card

    1. Map it: an excess is added → some reacts with the solid, the rest is left over → the leftover is titrated.
    2. The central block: n(excess reagent added) = c × V ÷ 1000.
    3. Titration: n(titrant) = c × V ÷ 1000.
    4. Titration ratio → leftover in the 25.0 cm³ portion.
    5. × 10 → leftover in the whole flask.
    6. Block − leftover = the piece that reacted with the solid.
    7. Reaction ratio → n(solid).
    8. Finish: mass and % purity, or Mr and identify.
