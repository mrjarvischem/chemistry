Optical isomerism

A complex has optical isomers if its mirror image cannot be superimposed on it. This happens with octahedral complexes containing two or three bidentate ligands, but only when there is no plane of symmetry.

Cis–trans isomerism

Cis: two identical ligands next to each other (90°). Trans: two identical ligands opposite each other (180°). It happens in octahedral and square planar complexes, but not in tetrahedral ones.
