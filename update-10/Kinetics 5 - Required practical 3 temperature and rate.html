1. Required Practical 3: the effect of temperature on rate

Na₂S₂O₃(aq) + 2HCl(aq) → 2NaCl(aq) + SO₂(g) + S(s) + H₂O(l)

  1. Measure 10 cm³ of sodium thiosulfate and 10 cm³ of HCl into separate test tubes. Warm both in a water bath to the chosen temperature.
  2. Put a conical flask on a paper cross. Pour in both solutions and start the timer as they mix.
  3. Look down through the flask. Stop the timer when the cross disappears.
  4. Measure the temperature at the start and end and take the mean.
  5. Repeat at 4–5 more temperatures. Keep volumes, concentrations, flask and cross the same.

3. Uncertainty and evaluation

% uncertainty = uncertaintymeasured value × 100
  • Reaction time is about ± 0.5 s whatever the time. So a short time has a much bigger % uncertainty. At high temperatures the results get less reliable.
  • A thermometer reading ±0.5 °C. Each value is read twice (start and end), but you take a mean.
  • When a value is found from a difference of two readings, add the two uncertainties.
