Equilibrium (Kp) 7 – Effect of temperature and catalysts on Kp

AQA A-level Chemistry · Equilibrium constant Kp walkthrough

1. Temperature is the only thing that changes Kp

  • Raising the temperature shifts the equilibrium in the endothermic direction (to absorb the extra heat).
  • If the forward reaction is exothermic: higher T → equilibrium moves left → less product, more reactant → Kp decreases.
  • If the forward reaction is endothermic: higher T → equilibrium moves right → Kp increases.

The mark scheme wants three things: the effect on Kp, the direction the equilibrium shifts (endothermic direction / to the left or right), and why (to oppose the temperature increase).

Worked example with the mark scheme

June 2018 Paper 1 Q2.4 (3 marks)

N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g), ΔH = −92 kJ mol⁻¹. State the effect, if any, of an increase in temperature on the value of Kp. Justify your answer.

  1. M1 Kp decreases (if you say increase or no change, you get 0 marks)
  2. M2 The equilibrium shifts in the endothermic direction, which is to the left
  3. M3 to oppose the increase in temperature (to reduce the temperature)

2. Past-paper practice

QUICK CHECK 1 June 2019 Paper 1 Q7.3 (2 marks)

2SO₃(g) ⇌ 2SO₂(g) + O₂(g). At 1050 K, Kp = 7.62 × 10⁵ Pa. At 500 K, Kp = 3.94 × 10⁴ Pa. Explain how this information can be used to deduce that the forward reaction is endothermic.

M1 Kp is higher at the higher temperature.
M2 So at higher temperature more dissociation occurs / more products form / the equilibrium shifts to the right. Raising the temperature favours the endothermic direction, so the forward reaction is endothermic. (M2 depends on M1.)

3. Catalysts

A catalyst speeds up the forward and backward reactions equally. Equilibrium is reached faster, but the position of equilibrium and the value of Kp do not change.

QUICK CHECK 4 November 2020 Paper 1 Q6.5 (2 marks)

CO(g) + 2H₂(g) ⇌ CH₃OH(g). State the effect, if any, of adding a catalyst on the value of Kp. Explain your answer.

M1 No effect.
M2 It increases the rate of the forward and backward reactions equally / by the same amount, OR it does not affect the position of equilibrium. (Allow: a catalyst does not appear in the Kp expression; only temperature affects Kp. Ignore 'catalysts increase the rate of reaction' on its own.)

4. Summary: what changes what?

The forward reaction is exothermic and has fewer moles of gas on the right, like N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃. Choose the effect of each change, then check.
