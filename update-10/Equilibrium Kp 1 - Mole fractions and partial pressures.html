Equilibrium (Kp) 1 – Mole fractions and partial pressures

AQA A-level Chemistry · Equilibrium constant Kp walkthrough

1. Mole fraction: what share of the gas is each substance?

In a mixture of gases, the mole fraction of a gas is the fraction of all the gas molecules that are that gas.

mole fraction of A = moles of Atotal moles of gas
  • Mole fractions have no units and are always between 0 and 1.
  • The mole fractions of all the gases in a mixture add up to 1.
  • Only count gases in the total. Ignore any solids or liquids.

Interactive: build a gas mixture

Change the amount of each gas. The bar shows each gas's share of the total.

Common mistake: dividing by the starting total. Always use the total moles of gas at equilibrium. The total often changes as the reaction happens.

2. Partial pressure: each gas's share of the pressure

Each gas in a mixture pushes on the container walls as if it were there alone. The pressure it exerts is its partial pressure, written p(A).

p(A) = mole fraction of A × total pressure

The partial pressures add up to the total pressure: P = p(A) + p(B) + p(C) + …

Partial pressures have the same units as the total pressure (usually kPa or Pa).

Worked example with the mark scheme

June 2018 Paper 1 Q2.1 (3 marks)

Nitrogen and hydrogen were mixed in a 1:3 mole ratio and left to reach equilibrium: N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g). At equilibrium the total pressure was 150 kPa and the mole fraction of NH₃ was 0.80. Calculate the partial pressure of each gas.

  1. p(NH₃) = 0.80 × 150 = 120 kPa
  2. N₂ and H₂ share the rest: 150 − 120 = 30 kPa
  3. N₂ and H₂ started in a 1:3 ratio and react in a 1:3 ratio, so they are still in a 1:3 ratio.
  4. p(N₂) = ¼ × 30 = 7.5 kPa; p(H₂) = ¾ × 30 = 22.5 kPa
  5. Mark scheme alternative: mole fractions 0.05 and 0.15, so 0.05 × 150 and 0.15 × 150.

3. Past-paper practice

QUICK CHECK 1 June 2019 Paper 3 Q11

2SO₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2SO₃(g). 2.00 mol of SO₂ is mixed with 2.00 mol of O₂. The total amount of the three gases at equilibrium is 3.40 mol. What is the mole fraction of SO₃ in the equilibrium mixture?

If x mol of O₂ react, the total falls by x (3 mol of gas become 2 mol).
4.00 − x = 3.40, so x = 0.60 → SO₃ formed = 2x = 1.20 mol
Mole fraction SO₃ = 1.20 ÷ 3.40 = 0.353 (answer B)
QUICK CHECK 2 June 2017 Paper 3 Q28

A 385 cm³ sample of carbon dioxide at 100 kPa and 25 °C was mixed with 2.89 × 10⁻² mol of argon. R = 8.31 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹. What is the mole fraction of carbon dioxide in the mixture?

n(CO₂) = pV ÷ RT = (100 000 × 385 × 10⁻⁶) ÷ (8.31 × 298) = 0.0155 mol
Total = 0.0155 + 0.0289 = 0.0444 mol
Mole fraction = 0.0155 ÷ 0.0444 = 0.35 (answer A)
QUICK CHECK 3 June 2023 Paper 3 Q28

C₄H₁₀ + 6½O₂ → 4CO₂ + 5H₂O. What is the mole fraction of butane in a mixture of butane and oxygen with the minimum amount of oxygen needed for complete combustion?

1 mol butane needs 6.5 mol O₂. Total = 7.5 mol.
Mole fraction = 1 ÷ 7.5 = 0.133 (answer A)
