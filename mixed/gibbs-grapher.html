ΔS must be in kJ K⁻¹ mol⁻¹ to match ΔH, so divide J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹ by 1000. Temperature is always in kelvin.
The model assumes ΔH and ΔS stay constant as temperature changes. Feasible (ΔG ≤ 0) says nothing about rate: a feasible reaction can still be very slow if its activation energy is high.
