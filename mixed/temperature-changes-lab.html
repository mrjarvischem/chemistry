Method
  1. Measure 30 cm³ of dilute hydrochloric acid into a polystyrene cup. Stand the cup in a beaker so it does not fall over.
  2. Record the temperature of the acid.
  3. Add 5 cm³ of sodium hydroxide solution, put on the lid and stir. Record the highest temperature reached.
  4. Keep adding portions until 50 cm³ has been added, recording the highest temperature each time.
  5. Plot highest temperature against volume of sodium hydroxide. Draw two lines of best fit and find where they cross.

Method
  1. Measure 25 cm³ of citric acid solution into a polystyrene cup standing in a beaker. Record the starting temperature.
  2. Weigh out a mass of sodium hydrogencarbonate. Add it to the acid, put on the lid and stir.
  3. Record the lowest temperature reached. Work out the decrease in temperature.
  4. Repeat with fresh acid and different masses of sodium hydrogencarbonate.
  5. Plot decrease in temperature against mass. Draw two lines of best fit and find where they cross.
C₆H₈O₇(aq) + 3NaHCO₃(s) → Na₃C₆H₅O₇(aq) + 3H₂O(l) + 3CO₂(g) · endothermic
