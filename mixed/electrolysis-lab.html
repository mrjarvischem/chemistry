Electrolysis – Virtual Lab

GCSE Chemistry Required Practical · Electrolysis of aqueous solutions with inert electrodes · Identify the products at each electrode
Method
  1. Pour the solution into the electrolysis cell. Fill two small test tubes with the solution and turn them upside down over the electrodes (or fill the Hofmann voltameter).
  2. Connect the electrodes to a low-voltage DC power supply. The electrode connected to the negative terminal is the cathode; the positive one is the anode.
  3. Switch on and watch both electrodes closely. Record what you see.
  4. Test any gas collected: lighted splint (hydrogen), glowing splint (oxygen), damp blue litmus paper (chlorine).
  5. Switch off, clean the electrodes and repeat with the other solutions.
Safety: chlorine is toxic – use a fume cupboard or keep the time short. Wear eye protection.
