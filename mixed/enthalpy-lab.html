Set up

Procedure

  1. Start the stopwatch and take readings (e.g. every 30 s) for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the reagent at a time you choose (e.g. 3 min 30 s) — don't record at that instant.
  3. Keep stirring and recording until the temperature has clearly been falling (or rising back) for several minutes.
  4. Plot the graph and extrapolate the cooling line back to the time of addition.

Set up

in place

Procedure

  1. Weigh the spirit burner with its cap on.
  2. Start the stopwatch, record the water temperature, then remove the cap and light the burner.
  3. Stir and record the temperature until it has risen by about 20–30 °C.
  4. Replace the cap to put out the flame, record the highest temperature, then reweigh the burner with its cap.
