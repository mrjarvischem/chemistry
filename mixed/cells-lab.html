E° values from the AQA data booklet. E under non-standard conditions is calculated with the Nernst equation, E = E° − (RT/nF) ln Q. The Nernst equation is not on the AQA specification, but it gives the same direction of change as the Le Chatelier argument you are expected to use. E° values are defined at 298 K; this model does not include the small change of E° itself with temperature, so temperature only acts through the (RT/nF) ln Q term. Spectator ions (sulfate, nitrate) are not shown in the particle view.
