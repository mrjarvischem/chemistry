Momentum

Every moving object has momentum. It depends on mass and velocity, and — like velocity — it is a vector.

p = m v Learn this — not on the equation sheet

p = momentum (kg m/s), m = mass (kg), v = velocity (m/s). Momentum in the opposite direction is negative.

Conservation of momentum

In a closed system, the total momentum before an event is equal to the total momentum after the event.

Changes in momentum Physics only

F = m Δv ÷ Δt Given on the equation sheet

The force equals the rate of change of momentum. For the same change in momentum, a longer time means a smaller force.

Time to stop the 70 kg passenger from 15 m/s: s
Seat belts, air bags, crumple zones, cycle helmets and cushioned playground surfaces all increase the time taken for the change in momentum, so they reduce the force on the person and the risk of injury.

Exam practice

Lesson summary

  • p = m v (learn it), in kg m/s. Momentum is a vector.
  • In a closed system, total momentum before = total momentum after.
  • Explosions: total momentum before is zero, so the parts move off with equal and opposite momenta.
  • Physics only: F = m Δv ÷ Δt. Safety features increase the time → reduce the force.
