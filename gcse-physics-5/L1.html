Lesson 1 · Scalars, vectors and types of force

AQA GCSE Physics / Combined Science · Forces 4.5.1.1–4.5.1.2 · about 25 minutes

Scalars and vectors

Scalar

Has magnitude (size) only.
e.g. distance, speed, mass, time, temperature, energy.

Vector

Has magnitude and direction.
e.g. displacement, velocity, force, acceleration, momentum, weight.

Vectors are drawn as arrows

Force size: N
Direction: °

The length of the arrow shows the magnitude; the direction of the arrow shows the direction.

Contact and non-contact forces

A force is a push or pull that acts on an object because of an interaction with another object. Force is a vector, measured in newtons (N).

Contact forces

The objects are physically touching.
friction · air resistance · tension · normal contact force (reaction) · upthrust

Non-contact forces

The objects are physically separated.
gravitational force · electrostatic force · magnetic force

Identifying forces

Pick a situation and reveal the forces acting on the object.

Forces come in pairs between two objects. Each force in an interaction acts on a different object — you’ll meet this again with Newton’s third law.

Exam practice

Lesson summary

  • Scalar: magnitude only. Vector: magnitude and direction. Vectors are drawn as arrows.
  • Force: a push or pull from an interaction, measured in newtons (N).
  • Contact: friction, air resistance, tension, normal contact, upthrust.
  • Non-contact: gravitational, electrostatic, magnetic.
