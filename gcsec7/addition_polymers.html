Walkthrough 7 · Addition polymerisation (chemistry only)

AQA GCSE Chemistry 4.7.3.1 · Chemistry only — not Combined Science · about 25 minutes

Addition polymerisation CHEMISTRY ONLY

Alkenes can be used to make polymers such as poly(ethene) and poly(propene). Many small molecules called monomers join together to form very large molecules.

In addition polymerisation the C=C double bonds open and the monomers join in a long chain. The polymer is the only product, and the repeating unit has the same atoms as the monomer.

Summary

  • Monomers (alkenes) join to form a polymer; the C=C opens.
  • Addition polymer is the only product; the repeating unit has the same atoms as the monomer.
  • Name: poly(monomer), e.g. poly(ethene), poly(propene).
