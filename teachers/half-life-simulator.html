Key ideas

  • Decay is random. You cannot predict which nucleus will decay next, or when a particular nucleus will decay.
  • Half-life is the time taken for the number of undecayed nuclei in a sample to halve. It is also the time for the count rate (or activity) to fall to half its starting value.
  • Each isotope has its own half-life, and it stays the same however much of the sample is left.
  • After n half-lives the fraction remaining is 1 ÷ 2n. Small samples stray further from this than large ones.
