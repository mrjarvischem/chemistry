Teacher notes

Variables

  • Independent: type (or concentration) of antiseptic or antibiotic.
  • Dependent: diameter, and so area, of the clear zone where bacteria have not grown.
  • Control: species of bacteria, volume of culture spread, size of disc, incubation temperature and time.

Aseptic technique this models

  • Sterilise Petri dishes and agar before use, and pass the inoculating loop through a flame.
  • Lift the lid only slightly, secure it with tape without sealing all the way round, and store the plate upside down.
  • Incubate at a maximum of 25 °C in school, to make it less likely that harmful bacteria grow.

Questions to draw out

  • Why is there a disc soaked in water?
  • Why compare areas and not just diameters? Calculate one area by hand with πr².
  • Switch aseptic technique off and incubate. Where did the extra colonies come from, and why do they make the result invalid?
  • Why is the plate not incubated at 37 °C in school?
  • Run several plates. Is the order of effectiveness always the same?
