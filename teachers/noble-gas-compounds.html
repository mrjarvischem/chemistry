4. Xenon chemistry: routes between the compounds

Step 1. Xenon + fluorine

The product depends on how much fluorine is used. Choose the Xe : F2 ratio.

Step 2. Add water

2XeF2 + 2H2O → 2Xe + O2 + 4HF

Slow. Xenon is reduced back to the element.

6XeF4 + 12H2O → 2XeO3 + 4Xe + 3O2 + 24HF

Disproportionation: Xe(+4) goes to Xe(+6) and Xe(0).

XeF6 + H2O → XeOF4 + 2HF

A little water.

XeF6 + 3H2O → XeO3 + 6HF

Excess water. Not a redox reaction: xenon stays +6.

Step 3. Up to xenon(+8)

XeO3 + OH⁻ → HXeO4⁻

Xenate ion, in alkali.

2HXeO4⁻ + 2OH⁻ → XeO6⁴⁻ + Xe + O2 + 2H2O

Disproportionation gives the perxenate ion, Xe(+8).

Ba2XeO6 + 2H2SO4 → XeO4 + 2BaSO4 + 2H2O

Cold concentrated sulfuric acid releases XeO4 gas.
