What is happening now?

When blood glucose is too high
Blood glucose rises above normal
Pancreas detects the change
Pancreas releases insulin
Glucose moves from blood into cells; liver and muscles store it as glycogen
Blood glucose falls back to normal
When blood glucose is too low (Higher tier)
Blood glucose falls below normal
Pancreas detects the change
Pancreas releases glucagon
Liver converts glycogen into glucose and releases it
Blood glucose rises back to normal
