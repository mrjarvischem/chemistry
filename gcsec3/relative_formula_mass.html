Relative formula mass and percentage by mass

AQA GCSE 4.3.1.2 / Trilogy 5.3.1.2

Key points

  • Click any element in the table to look at it.
  • The relative atomic mass, Ar, is the top number in each box. It compares the mass of the atom with other atoms.
  • The bottom number is the atomic (proton) number. Do not use it for masses.
  • Ar has no units.

Level

Key points

  • The relative formula mass, Mr, is the sum of the relative atomic masses of all the atoms shown in the formula.
  • A small number after a symbol tells you how many of that atom.
  • A number after a bracket multiplies everything inside the bracket.

Method

  • Work out the mass of the atoms you do know.
  • Subtract this from the Mr to find the mass left for X.
  • If there is more than one X in the formula, divide by the number of X atoms.
  • Find the element with that Ar in the periodic table.

Method

  • Find the Mr of the compound.
  • Find the total mass of the element: number of atoms × Ar.
  • Percentage by mass = mass of the element ÷ Mr × 100.
