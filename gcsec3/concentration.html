Concentration of solutions

AQA GCSE 4.3.2.5 and 4.3.4 / Trilogy 5.3.2.5 (mol/dm³ is HT only)

Make a solution

Mass of solute5 g
Volume of solution250 cm³

Key points

  • Concentration is the mass of solute dissolved in a given volume of solution.
  • It is measured in grams per dm³ (g/dm³).
  • More solute in the same volume: more concentrated.
  • Same solute in a bigger volume: less concentrated.
  • 1 dm³ = 1000 cm³. To change cm³ to dm³, divide by 1000.

Method

  • Convert the volume to dm³ first: cm³ ÷ 1000.
  • Concentration (g/dm³) = mass of solute (g) ÷ volume of solution (dm³).
  • To find a mass: mass = concentration × volume in dm³.

Method

  • Concentration can also be given in mol/dm³: moles of solute in each dm³ of solution.
  • Concentration (mol/dm³) = moles ÷ volume in dm³.
  • To change g/dm³ to mol/dm³, divide by the Mr. To change mol/dm³ to g/dm³, multiply by the Mr.
