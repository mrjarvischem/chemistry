Reacting masses

AQA GCSE 4.3.2.2 and 4.3.2.3 / Trilogy 5.3.2.2 and 5.3.2.3 (HT only)

Key points

  • The coefficients (big numbers) in a balanced equation tell you the ratio in moles in which substances react and are made.
  • 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO means 2 mol of magnesium react with 1 mol of oxygen to make 2 mol of magnesium oxide.
  • To change from one substance to another: divide by its own coefficient (big number), then multiply by the coefficient (big number) of the substance you want.

Method

  • Mass to moles for the substance you know: moles = mass ÷ Mr.
  • Use the ratio from the equation to find the moles of the substance you want.
  • Moles to mass: mass = moles × Mr.
  • Never multiply an Mr by the coefficient (big number) in the equation.

Method

  • Convert every mass to moles: moles = mass ÷ Mr.
  • Divide each number of moles by the smallest one to get a simple ratio.
  • If the ratio is not whole numbers, multiply them all up (for example 1 : 1.5 becomes 2 : 3).
  • The whole numbers are the balancing numbers.
