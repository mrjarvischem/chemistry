What is happening

  1. Short wavelength radiation from the Sun passes through the atmosphere.
  2. The Earth's surface absorbs it and warms up.
  3. The warm surface emits longer wavelength infrared radiation.
  4. Greenhouse gases absorb the infrared and re-emit it in all directions. Some goes back to the surface, keeping the Earth warm.

Possible effects of climate change

  • Sea level rise, flooding and coastal erosion
  • More frequent and severe storms
  • Changes in the amount, timing and distribution of rainfall
  • Temperature and water stress for humans and wildlife
  • Changes in the food-producing capacity of some regions
  • Changes to where wildlife species can live
