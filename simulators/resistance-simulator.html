Teacher notes

Length of a wire

  • Independent: length of wire between the crocodile clips. Dependent: resistance (p.d. ÷ current). Control: the wire's material and thickness, and its temperature.
  • Method: connect the ammeter in series and the voltmeter in parallel with the wire. Move the flying lead along the wire, recording p.d. and current at each length.
  • Why does the line of best fit miss the origin? The clips and leads add a small contact resistance, a zero error.
  • Why keep the current small and switch off between readings? Try the thick wire at 10 cm and watch the current.
  • Compare the three thicknesses: which has the steepest line, and why?
  • Why do the readings scatter about the line? The meters only read to two decimal places.

Series and parallel

  • Measure each resistor alone, then the pair in series, then in parallel, and calculate the resistance each time.
  • Series: the total is the sum of the two resistances.
  • Parallel: the total is less than the smaller of the two resistances, because there are now two paths for the current.

The data are simulated. The wire is modelled on constantan (0.27, 0.38 and 0.56 mm diameter) with a 1.5 V cell and about 0.25 Ω of contact resistance.
