Writing nuclei

Top number: mass number (protons + neutrons).
Bottom number: atomic number (protons).
Uranium-238 has 92 protons and 238 − 92 = 146 neutrons.

The decay machine

Pick a nucleus and let it decay. Watch what happens to the mass number and atomic number.

Alpha decay

Mass number − 4, atomic number − 2. A new element is formed, two places back in the periodic table.

Beta decay

Mass number unchanged, atomic number + 1 (a neutron became a proton). A new element, one place forward.

Gamma emission doesn’t change the mass or the charge of the nucleus — it just gets rid of extra energy. Neutron emission lowers the mass number by 1; the element stays the same.

Lesson summary

  • Top = mass number; bottom = atomic number. Both must balance.
  • Alpha (⁴₂He): mass − 4, atomic number − 2.
  • Beta (⁰₋₁e): mass unchanged, atomic number + 1.
  • Gamma: no change in mass or charge. Neutron: mass − 1.
