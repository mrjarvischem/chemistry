Series circuits

In a series circuit the components are connected one after another in a single loop. Change the resistors and watch the meters.

Supply p.d. = V
R₁ = Ω
R₂ = Ω
In series:
• the current is the same through every component
• the supply p.d. is shared: Vtotal = V₁ + V₂
• the total resistance is the sum: Rtotal = R₁ + R₂

Who gets the biggest share?

The current is the same in both resistors, so by V = I R the bigger resistance gets the bigger share of the p.d. Double the resistance → double the share.

Use the sliders in section 1 — the bars follow.

Adding more components

Adding another resistor in series increases the total resistance, so the current decreases. Lamps in series get dimmer as you add more. And if one lamp breaks, the circuit is broken and they all go out.

Exam practice

Lesson summary

  • Series: one loop. Current is the same everywhere.
  • The supply p.d. is shared between components: Vtotal = V₁ + V₂ + …
  • Rtotal = R₁ + R₂ + … Adding resistors in series reduces the current.
  • Bigger resistance → bigger share of the p.d.
