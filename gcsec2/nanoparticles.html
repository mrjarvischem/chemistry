1 nm = 1 × 10⁻⁹ m. A nanoparticle contains only a few hundred atoms. Nanoscience is the study of structures that are 1 to 100 nm in size.

Key points

  • Surface area of a cube = 6 × side². Volume = side³.
  • As the side of a cube decreases by a factor of 10, the surface area to volume ratio increases by a factor of 10.

Why are the properties different?

  • Nanoparticles may have properties different from those of the same material in bulk because of their high surface area to volume ratio.
  • A much bigger fraction of the atoms is at the surface, where reactions happen.
  • So smaller quantities are needed to be effective than for materials with normal particle sizes.

Possible risks

  • Nanoparticles are small enough to be breathed in, or to pass through the skin, and may get into the bloodstream and cells.
  • They are new, so the long-term effects on health are not yet known.
  • They may be washed into rivers and harm the environment.
