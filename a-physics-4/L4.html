Lesson 4 · Series and parallel circuits, and power

AQA A-level Physics 3.5.1.4 · about 25 minutes

Resistors in series and parallel

Series: RT = R1 + R2 + … — same current; p.d. shared.
Parallel: 1/RT = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + … — same p.d.; current shared. RT is less than the smallest resistor.
Supply: V
R₁: Ω
R₂: Ω
Kirchhoff’s first law (conservation of charge): total current into a junction = total current out.
Kirchhoff’s second law (conservation of energy): in any closed loop, the sum of the emfs = the sum of the p.d.s.

Electrical power and energy

P = IV = I²R = V²/R · energy E = IVt
Choose the version of P that uses the quantities you know. When comparing resistors in series (same I) use I²R; in parallel (same V) use V²/R.

Lesson summary

  • Series R add; parallel 1/R add.
  • Series: same I, V shared. Parallel: same V, I shared.
  • Kirchhoff 1: charge conserved at junctions; Kirchhoff 2: energy conserved round loops.
  • P = IV = I²R = V²/R; E = IVt.
