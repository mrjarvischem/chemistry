The spin of the hydrogen nucleus (a proton) flips, not an electron. Electrons matter because they shield the nucleus from B₀: more electron density means a smaller field felt, a smaller ΔE, a lower frequency and a smaller δ. Electronegative atoms such as O pull electron density away, so those H are deshielded and appear at higher δ.
