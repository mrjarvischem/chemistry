Spectra are modelled from typical liquid-film peak positions and use the AQA data sheet ranges. Colours are false colours: violet for high-energy (high wavenumber) photons through to red for low-energy ones. Modern FTIR instruments measure every wavenumber at once and sort them out mathematically; the scanning beam here shows the same idea one wavenumber at a time.
