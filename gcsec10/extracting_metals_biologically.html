AQA GCSE Chemistry 4.10.1.4 · Combined Science 5.10.1.4 · Higher tier only · about 20 minutes

Why are new methods needed? HT

The Earth's resources of metal ores are limited. Copper ores with a high proportion of copper are running out, so new ways of extracting copper from low-grade ores are being developed. These methods avoid traditional mining, which involves digging, moving and disposing of large amounts of rock.

Getting copper from the compounds

Both methods produce copper compounds (in the ash or the leachate). The metal is then obtained by:

Displacement with scrap iron

Iron is more reactive than copper, so it displaces it. Scrap iron is cheap.
{Fe + CuSO4 -> FeSO4 + Cu}

Electrolysis

Copper ions go to the cathode and are reduced to copper.
{Cu^2+ + 2e^- -> Cu}

Summary

  • Phytomining: plants absorb metal compounds; harvested and burned; ash contains metal compounds.
  • Bioleaching: bacteria produce leachate solutions containing metal compounds.
  • Copper is then obtained by displacement with scrap iron or by electrolysis.
  • Used for low-grade ores; avoid large-scale mining but are slow.
