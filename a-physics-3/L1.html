Lesson 1 · Scalars, vectors and equilibrium

AQA A-level Physics 3.4.1.1 · about 25 minutes

Scalars and vectors

Scalar

Magnitude (size) only.
e.g. distance, speed, mass, time, energy, temperature, density, power.

Vector

Magnitude and direction.
e.g. displacement, velocity, acceleration, force, weight, momentum.

Adding and resolving vectors

Equilibrium of coplanar forces

An object is in equilibrium when the resultant force is zero (and the resultant moment is zero — Lesson 2). For three forces, they form a closed triangle when drawn tip to tail.

A picture hanging from two strings

Weight: N
String angle to horizontal: °
In equilibrium, resolve in two perpendicular directions and set each sum to zero: “up = down” and “left = right”. Smaller string angles give much larger tensions.

Exam practice

Lesson summary

  • Scalars: magnitude only. Vectors: magnitude and direction.
  • Add vectors tip to tail; perpendicular: R = √(A² + B²), tan θ = B/A.
  • Resolve: F cos θ and F sin θ. On a slope: mg sin θ along, mg cos θ perpendicular.
  • Equilibrium: resultant force zero; three forces form a closed triangle.
