Lesson 7 · Work, energy, power and conservation of energy

AQA A-level Physics 3.4.1.7 – 3.4.1.8 · about 25 minutes

Work done and power

W = Fs cos θ (θ = angle between force and displacement) · P = ΔW/Δt · P = Fv · efficiency = useful output power ÷ input power
Force: N
Angle to horizontal: °
Distance: m
The area under a force–displacement graph is the work done. A force perpendicular to the motion (e.g. weight for horizontal motion) does no work.

Conservation of energy

Ek = ½mv² · ΔEp = mgΔh · Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transferred.
Start height: m
Friction (work done against resistance): %
With no losses, mgh = ½mv² → v = √(2gh) — independent of mass. If there is friction, Ep lost = Ek gained + work done against friction.

Exam practice

Lesson summary

  • W = Fs cos θ (J); area under F–s graph = work.
  • P = W/t = Fv; efficiency = useful output ÷ input.
  • Eₖ = ½mv², ΔEₚ = mgΔh; energy is conserved — losses are work done against resistive forces.
