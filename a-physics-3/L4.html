Lesson 4 · Projectile motion

AQA A-level Physics 3.4.1.4 · about 25 minutes

Horizontal and vertical motion are independent

Two balls: one dropped, one fired horizontally at the same moment. Which lands first?

Ignoring air resistance: horizontal velocity is constant (no horizontal force); vertical motion has acceleration g downwards. Treat them separately — time is the link between them.

Projectiles launched at an angle

Speed: m s⁻¹
Angle: °
Launch height: m

Method

  1. Resolve the launch velocity: ux = u cos θ, uy = u sin θ.
  2. Vertical: use SUVAT with a = −g to find the time of flight (or max height).
  3. Horizontal: range = ux × time.
With air resistance the range and maximum height are both smaller, and the path is no longer symmetrical — it comes down more steeply. (Only a qualitative treatment is needed.)

Exam practice

Lesson summary

  • Horizontal and vertical motion are independent; horizontal velocity constant (no air resistance), vertical acceleration g.
  • Resolve: u cos θ and u sin θ. Use vertical motion to find time; range = horizontal velocity × time.
  • Air resistance reduces range and height and makes the path asymmetric.
