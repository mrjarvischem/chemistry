Lesson 4 · Coulomb’s law and electric field strength

AQA A-level Physics 3.7.3.1–3.7.3.2 · about 25 minutes

Coulomb’s law

F = Q₁Q₂ / 4πε₀r² for point charges (ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² F m⁻¹). Like charges repel, unlike attract. In air, use ε₀ (air ≈ vacuum).
Compare with gravity: for two protons, Fe/Fg ≈ 10³⁶ — gravity is negligible for subatomic particles.

Electric field strength

E = F/Q (N C⁻¹) — force per unit positive charge. Radial: E = Q/4πε₀r². Uniform (parallel plates): E = V/d (V m⁻¹).

Exam practice

ε₀ = 8.85 × 10−12 F m−1; e = 1.60 × 10−19 C; me = 9.11 × 10−31 kg.

Lesson summary

  • F = Q₁Q₂/4πε₀r² (inverse square).
  • E = F/Q; radial E = Q/4πε₀r²; uniform E = V/d.
  • Charge in uniform field: constant force → parabolic path.
  • Work done accelerating through p.d. V: W = QV.
