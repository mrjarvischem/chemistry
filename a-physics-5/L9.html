Lesson 9 · Magnetic flux and electromagnetic induction

AQA A-level Physics 3.7.5.3–3.7.5.4 · about 25 minutes

Magnetic flux and flux linkage

Flux Φ = BA (weber, Wb) when B ⟂ A. Flux linkage NΦ = BAN cos θ (Wb-turns), θ = angle between B and the normal to the coil.
RP11: a search coil connected to an oscilloscope, in the field of an AC solenoid. Rotate the coil: the induced emf peak ∝ cos θ.

Faraday’s and Lenz’s laws

Faraday: induced emf = rate of change of flux linkage, ε = NΔΦ/Δt. Lenz: the induced current opposes the change that causes it (energy conservation).

A rod of length l moving at v across B: ε = Blv.

A coil rotating in a field (generator)

NΦ = BAN cos ωt → ε = BANω sin ωt; peak emf ε₀ = BANω. Emf is maximum when the coil plane is parallel to B (flux linkage zero, changing fastest).
ω: rad s⁻¹

Exam practice

Lesson summary

  • Φ = BA; NΦ = BAN cos θ.
  • ε = NΔΦ/Δt (Faraday); induced current opposes change (Lenz).
  • ε = BANω sin ωt; peak when coil plane ∥ B.
