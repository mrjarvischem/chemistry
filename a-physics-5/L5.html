Lesson 5 · Electric potential and comparing fields

AQA A-level Physics 3.7.3.3, 3.7.1 · about 25 minutes

Absolute electric potential

V = work done per unit positive charge moving it from infinity to the point. V = Q/4πε₀r. Positive near a + charge, negative near a − charge; zero at infinity. ΔW = QΔV; E = ΔV/Δr (magnitude = gradient of V–r graph).

r: m

Q = 5.0 nC. Area under E–r graph = ΔV.

Comparing gravitational and electric fields

GravitationalElectric
ForceF = GMm/r²F = Q₁Q₂/4πε₀r²

PotentialV = −GM/rV = Q/4πε₀r

Both are inverse-square laws, and both have potential ∝ 1/r.

Lesson summary

  • V = Q/4πε₀r; ΔW = QΔV.
  • E = gradient of V–r graph; ΔV = area under E–r graph.
  • Equipotentials ⟂ field lines.
  • Gravity always attractive; electric forces can be either.
