Lesson 7 · Capacitor charging and discharging (RP9)

AQA A-level Physics 3.7.4.4 · about 25 minutes

Exponential discharge

Discharging through R: Q = Q₀e−t/RC (same form for V and I). Time constant τ = RC — time to fall to 1/e ≈ 37%. Half-life: T½ = 0.69RC.
R: kΩ
C: μF
mode:
Charging: Q = Q₀(1 − e−t/RC) and V likewise, but current decays: I = I₀e−t/RC.

Required practical 9: finding RC

Charge C to V₀, then discharge through R, recording V every 10 s (or use a data logger). Take logs: ln V = ln V₀ − t/RC. A graph of ln V against t is a straight line of gradient −1/RC.

Exam practice

Lesson summary

  • Discharge: Q, V, I ∝ e−t/RC.
  • Charge: Q, V = max × (1 − e−t/RC); I decays.
  • τ = RC (37%); T½ = 0.69RC.
  • ln V vs t: gradient −1/RC.
