Lesson 8 · Magnetic force on currents and moving charges

AQA A-level Physics 3.7.5.1–3.7.5.2 · about 25 minutes

Force on a current-carrying wire

F = BIl when the wire is perpendicular to B. Magnetic flux density B (tesla, T): force per unit current per unit length on a wire at right angles to the field. Direction: Fleming’s left-hand rule (First finger Field, seCond finger Current, thuMb Motion).
RP10: wire clamped across a magnet on a top-pan balance. Change in reading × g = F. Plot F against I: gradient = Bl.

Charged particles in a magnetic field

F = BQv, always perpendicular to velocity → no work done, speed constant → circular path. BQv = mv²/r → r = mv/BQ.

Lesson summary

  • F = BIl (⟂), Fleming’s LHR.
  • F = BQv; circular motion, r = mv/BQ.
  • Magnetic force does no work on a moving charge.
  • RP10: F vs I on a balance gives B.
