Lesson 6 · Capacitance, dielectrics and stored energy

AQA A-level Physics 3.7.4.1–3.7.4.3 · about 25 minutes

Capacitance

C = Q/V — charge stored per unit p.d. (farad, F = C V⁻¹). Parallel plates: C = Aε₀εr/d.
plate area A: cm²
separation d: mm
dielectric εr:

How a dielectric increases C

Polar molecules in the dielectric rotate to align with the field. Their own field opposes the applied field, so the net field (and p.d.) between the plates is reduced for the same charge. Since C = Q/V, C increases. εr = C (with dielectric) ÷ C (vacuum).

Energy stored

E = area under Q–V graph = ½QV = ½CV² = ½Q²/C. Charging from a battery, the battery supplies QV — half is stored, half dissipated in the resistance.
