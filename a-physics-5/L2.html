Lesson 2 · Gravitational potential

AQA A-level Physics 3.7.2.3 · about 25 minutes

Gravitational potential

Gravitational potential V at a point = work done per unit mass to move a small mass from infinity to that point. V = −GM/r (J kg⁻¹). V = 0 at infinity, and is negative everywhere else.
r: RE

The gradient of the V–r graph gives the field strength: g = −ΔV/Δr.

Work done moving mass m between two points: ΔW = mΔV. Equipotentials are surfaces of equal potential — no work is done moving along them; they are perpendicular to field lines.

Escape velocity

To escape completely, an object needs enough kinetic energy to reach V = 0: ½mv² = GMm/r, so vesc = √(2GM/r) — independent of the object’s mass.

Exam practice

G = 6.67 × 10−11 N m² kg−2; ME = 5.97 × 1024 kg; RE = 6.37 × 106 m.

Lesson summary

  • V = −GM/r; zero at infinity, negative elsewhere.
  • ΔW = mΔV; g = −ΔV/Δr (gradient of V–r graph).
  • Equipotentials ⟂ field lines; no work done along them.
  • Escape velocity √(2GM/r).
